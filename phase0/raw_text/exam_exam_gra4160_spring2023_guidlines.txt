1.1
Assignment 1 Guidelines:
1. Load the Data:
• When loading data, make sure to import the required libraries and read the data from
the appropriate source.
• Since the dataset is distributed across four separate files, you need to concatenate these
into a single DataFrame using the appropriate Pandas functions.
• Be sure to inspect the structure and components of the DataFrame to ensure that the
import and concatenation were done correctly.
a. Comment on the completeness of the dataset:
– Use the appropriate Pandas functions to check for missing values in your DataFrame.
Clearly report any missing values and their count.
– Make comparisons across the four files in terms of data completeness.
– Propose a suitable method for handling missing values based on the situation (for ex-
ample, deleting missing data, replacing with the mean, median, mode etc.). Justify
your chosen method.
b. Decide on the variable:
– Consider whether all categories within the target variable are necessary for your
analysis or if simplification (to binary classification, for example) is more suitable.
– Justify your decision based on the nature of the dataset and the goals of your
analysis.
2. Convert Categorical Features:
• Identify the categorical variables in your dataset and use the appropriate Pandas methods
to convert them to categorical data types.
• Make sure to verify that the conversion was successful.
3. Feature Scaling:
• Identify the continuous variables in your dataset that need to be scaled.
• Choose a suitable feature scaling technique such as normalization or standardization.
Justify your choice by discussing why it is suitable for your data and the problem at
hand.
• Apply the chosen scaling technique to the selected features and verify its successful
application.
4. Feature Engineering:
a. Create New Features:
• Use your understanding of the data and the problem at hand to construct new features
that could potentially aid in predicting heart disease.
