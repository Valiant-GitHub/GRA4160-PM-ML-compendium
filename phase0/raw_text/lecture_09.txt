Stochastic gradient descent (SGD)
▶SGD is a method for minimizing a cost function by iteratively moving in the direction of
steepest descent as deﬁned by the negative of the gradient
▶In contrast to batch gradient descent, which computes the gradient of the cost function
over the entire dataset, SGD computes the gradient on a single training example at a
time
▶This makes SGD much faster than batch gradient descent, but it also means that SGD
is much noisier
▶The noise can be reduced by averaging the gradients over several training examples

===== [PAGE 43] =====
Dropout
▶Dropout is a regularization technique that randomly drops out (sets to zero) some of
the neurons in the network during training
▶This helps prevent the network from overﬁtting to the training data
▶Dropout can be seen as an ensemble of all the networks that can be formed by dropping
out diﬀerent neurons
▶Typical dropout rates are between 0.2 and 0.5, and dropout is only applied during
training, not at inference time
