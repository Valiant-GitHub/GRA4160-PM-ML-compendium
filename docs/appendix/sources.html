Sources

Source fidelity. Every claim, formula, code idiom, and hyperparameter on this site traces to one of the sources below, at a citable location (file + slide or cell). This page is the source registry; it grows as the build proceeds.

Source hierarchy

Tier 1 — Lecture material (canonical).

  • Lecture slides (10 PDFs, course_materials/Lecture slides/) — Vegard Høghaug Larsen, GRA 4160, spring 2026. Lectures 1–10. Cited by logical slide number (the n/N footer), since the decks are progressive-build.
  • Course notebooks — 20 lecture notebooks (course_materials/Lecture notebooks/) + 11 exercise notebooks (course_materials/Exercises and solutions(VHL)/). Cited by cell index. Notebooks win for code, hyperparameters, and dataset details; slides win for conceptual framing and notation.

Tier 2 — Reference texts.

  • ISL — An Introduction to Statistical Learning (Python ed.; James, Witten, Hastie, Tibshirani). Primary for Mode A depth. Explicitly cited on Lecture 9 (activation figure); the single-hidden-layer model matches ISL Eq. 10.1.
  • ESL — The Elements of Statistical Learning, 2nd ed. (Hastie, Tibshirani, Friedman, 2009). Primary for Mode B rigor and the formal notation anchor (GR1). The course’s primary textbook.

Lens (not a content source). Past exams — three single-page cover sheets, the Spring-2025 take-home notebook (representative format), and two guideline PDFs. Used only to frame the drill section and the Past-Exam Lens callouts; method content is never drawn from exams.

Phase-0 extracts (build provenance)

The per-source structured extracts produced in Phase 0 live in phase0/ (not part of the rendered site):

  • phase0/lecture_01..10_extract.md — one per lecture (topics, formulas, notation, emphasis).
  • phase0/notebook_*.md — one per lecture notebook (datasets, preprocessing, methods, hyperparameters, cell indices).
  • phase0/exercise_01..06_*.md — one per exercise topic (stub + solution).
  • phase0/exam_spring2025_notebook.md, phase0/guidelines_extract.md — the exam lens.
  • phase0/notation_table.md, phase0/concept_inventory.md, phase0/recurrence_map.md, phase0/disagreements.md, phase0/references.md — synthesis.

Cross-checks & disagreements

Formula cross-checks against ESL/ISL (R9), structural flags (R10), notebook code/prose mismatches, and dataset-availability notes (C7) are logged in phase0/disagreements.md and surfaced in callouts on the relevant pages. Two course datasets referenced by exercises (SMSSpamCollection.csv, bank-additional-full.csv) are not present locally; those drill pages document the method/idiom without fabricating outputs.

Per-method ESL/ISL section citations are added as each method’s math section is written (Phase 3).
